Questions, answered

The questions owners ask us most often before a scoping call.

What does a scoping call cover — and will I be pitched?

It covers the problem as you see it, what you've tried, and whether a diagnostic makes sense. You won't be pitched. If we're the wrong fit, we'll say so.

How does pricing work?

Every engagement is priced before it starts. The diagnostic is a fixed fee; projects are scoped from the findings. See fees →

Who actually does the work?

Signe or Teo leads every engagement in person. Associates join for process mapping and data work, introduced by name at scoping.

How much of my team's time will it take?

During a diagnostic, usually an hour with each team lead and a few short conversations with the people doing the work. We agree it up front.

How is the work reported?

A short written update every week and a working session every fortnight.

What happens if it isn't working?

Either side can pause at the end of any step. We'd rather stop early than finish something that isn't helping.

What happens to a description of my problem if I send one?

Only the two founding partners read it. It is kept for twelve months, then deleted. It is never shared.

Why are there no numbers in the case studies?

Results depend on the company and the people as much as on us. A percentage would suggest you can expect the same, so we describe what changed instead.

Why can't you show me client names?

Most engagements are confidential, often including the fact that they happened. We keep that promise on this site too.
