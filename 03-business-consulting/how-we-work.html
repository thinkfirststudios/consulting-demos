The engagement, honestly

Four steps, in order. You can stop after any of them.

  1. Scoping call

    A conversation about the problem as you see it. If we're the wrong fit, we'll say so.

  2. Diagnostic

    A fixed-fee look at how the work really moves: interviews, walk-throughs and the reports you already have.

  3. Written findings

    A short document in plain language: what we see, why it persists, what we'd do first.

  4. The work

    Only if you want it. Scoped from the findings, priced before it starts, reviewed as we go.

The details

What you can expect

Who does the work
Signe or Teo leads every engagement and is there in person. Associates join for process mapping and data work, introduced by name at scoping.
On site or remote
Diagnostics are mostly on site. Later work is usually a mix, agreed at the start.
How we report
A short written update every week and a working session every fortnight.
What you get at the end
The written findings, the agreed changes in place, and notes your team can keep using without us.
What happens if it isn't working
Either side can pause at the end of any step. We'd rather stop early than finish something that isn't helping.
What we need from you
An owner or senior sponsor, access to the people doing the work, and honesty about what has been tried before.
