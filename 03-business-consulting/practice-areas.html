Six practice areas

One small team across six areas, so the person who finds the problem is the person who helps fix it.

Operations review

What this is
How the work actually moves from enquiry to delivery, walked end to end with the people who do it.
A typical engagement
Two to four weeks on site and on calls, ending with written findings and a short list of first moves.
What we need from you
Access to the floor or the team, the reports you already run, and an hour with each team lead.
What it is not
Not a software implementation, and not a headcount exercise.

Strategy & planning

What this is
Turning what the owners already know into a plan the rest of the company can read, argue with and act on.
A typical engagement
A series of working sessions with the leadership team, then a plan on a few pages that names owners and decisions.
What we need from you
Leadership time — usually three half-days — and honesty about what has been tried before.
What it is not
Not a vision statement, a brand exercise or a hundred-slide deck.

Growth readiness

What this is
Checking whether the company can take on more work without the cracks widening — before the growth arrives.
A typical engagement
A diagnostic across sales, delivery and finance handoffs, with the pinch points ranked and explained.
What we need from you
Your pipeline as it is, your delivery calendar, and a frank view of where things already strain.
What it is not
Not lead generation, marketing or sales training.

Process redesign

What this is
Rebuilding one process that has grown by accident — with the people who run it, so it sticks.
A typical engagement
Mapping the current process, agreeing the new one together, then supporting the first weeks of running it.
What we need from you
The process owner's time, and permission to change how things are done.
What it is not
Not a manual that sits in a drawer.

Organisation & roles

What this is
Who decides what, who owns which handoff, and what changes as the founder steps back, including succession in family firms.
A typical engagement
Interviews, a draft of roles and decision rights, and facilitated sessions to settle the disagreements.
What we need from you
Candour from the owners and time with each person whose role is changing.
What it is not
Not recruitment, and not an HR policy rewrite.

Data & reporting

What this is
Getting the few numbers that matter in front of the people who need them, early enough to act on.
A typical engagement
Agreeing what to measure, tidying where it comes from, and setting up a weekly view people actually open.
What we need from you
Read access to the systems you use and someone on your side who knows them.
What it is not
