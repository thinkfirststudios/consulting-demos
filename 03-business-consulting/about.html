About Drawknife

A small operations and strategy consultancy in Portland, Oregon, established in 2014.

Our story

Two handles, one blade

Signe and Teo met on the same factory floor, working it from opposite ends — Signe in operations, Teo in finance. Over the next decade they each watched outside advisers arrive, present a long deck, and leave the company more or less as they found it.

They started Drawknife in 2014, in a shared office above a joinery on Wexley Street. The name is a joiner's tool: two handles, one blade, drawn toward you, taking off a thin shaving at a time. It is how they like to work — close to the material, a little at a time, with both hands.

The firm is still small on purpose. The partner who runs your scoping call is the partner who runs your engagement.
