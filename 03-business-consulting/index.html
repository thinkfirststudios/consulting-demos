Who we are

Signe and Teo started Drawknife after years on the inside of growing companies — one running operations floors, the other running the numbers. They had watched too many consulting decks get filed and forgotten.

So the firm works the other way round: find the problem first, write it down in plain language, and only then talk about fixing it.

How an engagement works

Four steps, in order

  1. Scoping call

    A conversation about the problem as you see it. If we're the wrong fit, we'll say so.

  2. Diagnostic

    A fixed-fee look at how the work really moves: interviews, walk-throughs and the reports you already have.

  3. Written findings

    A short document in plain language: what we see, why it persists, what we'd do first.

  4. The work

    Only if you want it. Scoped from the findings, priced before it starts, reviewed as we go.

Our work

Work, without the numbers

Case studies describe what changed in kind, not in number, and never name the client. One is withheld entirely.

Case 01 · Wholesale & distribution

Regional, family-owned

Situation
Sales promised delivery dates before the warehouse knew the order existed.
Approach
We walked order to dispatch with the people who handle it, and redrew the handoff with both teams in the room.
What changed — in kind, not in number
One short daily dispatch meeting replaced a day of chasing. Promises are checked before they are made.
What we'd do differently
Bring the drivers in earlier — they knew where it broke before anyone else did.

Case 03 · Manufacturing

Second-generation family firm

Situation
The founder wanted to step back, but every decision still came to him.
Approach
We wrote down the decisions he actually made, then agreed with the family and managers who would take each one.
What changed — in kind, not in number
The managers now run the weekly meeting. The founder attends, and mostly listens.
What we'd do differently
Start the conversation with the next generation sooner — it was the hardest part and it came last.
