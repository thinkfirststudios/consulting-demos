Help with IRS and state letters

Plain and procedural. No outcome is implied anywhere on this page.

Book an appointmentGet a quote

Most letters from the IRS or the state ask a simple question: a missing form, a figure that doesn't match, a payment that hasn't arrived. Reading it with someone who has seen the letter before usually makes the next step obvious.

Where a letter needs someone to speak for you, that has to be a person authorised to represent taxpayers before the IRS. Who that is at this practice is confirmed before launch.
