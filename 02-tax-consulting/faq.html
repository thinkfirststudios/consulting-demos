What does a return cost, and why?

It depends on which band your return falls in. The fee tables show each band, its conditions and the period the fees are honoured for.

Seven things, mostly to do with how many moving parts the return has. See what drives the price →

What if my return is more complex than quoted?

We stop, tell you why, and agree the new band in writing before doing the extra work.

Yes — always in writing, and always before any work starts.

What if I come late in the season?

We will tell you honestly whether we can take the work, and if not, who might.

What is the extension policy?

We file extensions for clients whose paperwork is still arriving, and finish the return once it is complete.

Why are there no refund figures on this site?

Because a refund depends entirely on your own facts. We describe what we prepare and how we review it; we don't promise an outcome.

Why are there no testimonials?

A tax testimonial usually gives away something about the client's own tax position. We add reviews only with written permission, and never with amounts.
