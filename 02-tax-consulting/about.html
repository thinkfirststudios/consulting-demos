About the practice

Two rooms above the hardware shop in 2011. The same street today, a few more desks.

Book an appointmentGet a quote

Ruth opened Spanworth in 2011, after years of doing returns at her kitchen table for neighbours who wanted someone to explain the form back to them. The first office was two rooms above the hardware shop on Alder Street.

Theo joined in 2016 to look after small businesses — the cafés, contractors and shops on the same street — and the practice moved downstairs into Suite 2. Marion runs the office and keeps the season calendar honest.

The idea hasn't changed: tell people the price first, ask for the right documents once, and go through the return with them before it is filed.
