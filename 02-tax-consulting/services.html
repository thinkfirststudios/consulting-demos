INDIVIDUAL RETURN

Individual return

Who it is for
Employees, retirees, freelancers with a side income, and households with dependants or a home sale.
What it includes
Federal and state preparation, a review meeting, e-file, and a copy of everything we filed.
What drives the price
Number of schedules, number of states, rental or self-employment income.

We prepare your federal and state return from the documents you send through the secure platform, then sit down with you to go through it line by line before anything is filed.

Not included: Bookkeeping, prior-year clean-up, and responding to notices — each priced separately.

Typical turnaround: About ten working days in season, once everything has arrived.

SMALL-BUSINESS RETURN

Small-business return

Who it is for
Sole proprietors, partnerships and S corporations, usually with fewer than 25 staff.
What it includes
The entity return, owner K-1s where they apply, and a year-end checklist for next season.
What drives the price
Entity type, number of owners, bookkeeping condition.

We work from your books, or help you get them into shape first, and prepare the business return alongside the owners' individual returns so the two agree.

Not included: Payroll filings and monthly bookkeeping, unless added as a separate engagement.

Typical turnaround: Two to three weeks in season, depending on the books.

Not taking new season work right now — returning clients can still book.

PLANNING

Year-round planning

Who it is for
People whose year is changing: a new business, a move between states, a marriage, a retirement.
What it includes
Four scheduled check-ins a year and a written summary after each one.
What drives the price
How many moving parts there are, and how often we meet.

Short, scheduled conversations through the year so that nothing arrives as a surprise in April. We explain what changed and what to keep.

Not included: Preparing the return itself — that is its own engagement.

Typical turnaround: Check-ins are booked a quarter ahead.

Not taking new season work right now — returning clients can still book.

REPRESENTATION

Help with IRS and state letters

Who it is for
Anyone who has received a letter from the IRS or the state and wants it read and explained.
What it includes
Reading the letter with you, explaining what it asks for, and agreeing the next step in writing.
What drives the price
What the letter is about, and how many years it touches.

Not included: Any outcome — no one can promise how the IRS or the state will respond.

Typical turnaround: A first meeting within a week of your call.

BOOKKEEPING

Bookkeeping

Who it is for
Small businesses we already prepare returns for.
What it includes
Monthly or quarterly reconciliation and a short report you can read in five minutes.
What drives the price
Number of accounts and transactions, and how tidy the records are.

Clean books make the return quicker and cheaper. We keep them up to date through the year so the spring work is review, not reconstruction.

Not included: Payroll processing.

Typical turnaround: Reports by the 15th of the following month.

Not taking new season work right now — returning clients can still book.

AMENDED & PRIOR YEARS

Amended and prior-year returns

Who it is for
People with years they never filed, or a filed return that needs correcting.
What it includes
Preparation of each missing or amended year, filed in the right order, with a plain summary.
What drives the price
How many years, and how complete the records are.

We start with what you have, request what is missing, and work through the years one at a time so you always know where things stand.

Not included: Settling any amount owed — that is between you and the IRS or the state.

Typical turnaround: Agreed per year at the first meeting.
