About us

Your questions first. Then a plan you can read.

Maren and Teodor opened Carrack Advisory in 2011 with a simple idea: every client should sit down with the person writing their plan, and every fee should be explained before any work begins.

Our process

Four steps, at your pace

  1. 1

    Discover

    A first conversation about where you are, what you want to change and the questions on your mind.

  2. 2

    Plan

    We write up a plan in plain English and go through it with you, including what it does not cover.

  3. 3

    Implement

    Agreed steps are put in place, at your pace, with every cost explained before anything happens.

  4. 4

    Review

    We meet regularly to revisit the plan as your life, work and family change.
