What are Form ADV Part 2A and Form CRS?

They are documents every prospective client of a registered adviser is entitled to read. Form ADV Part 2A describes the firm, its services, fees and conflicts; Form CRS is a short summary of the relationship. Read them on the disclosures page.

Do you prepare tax returns?

No. We plan with taxes in mind and work alongside your own accountant, who prepares and files your return.
