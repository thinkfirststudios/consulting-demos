About Carrack Advisory

A small planning practice on Corliss Street in Alderbrook, founded in 2011.

Our story

A practice built around the first conversation

Maren and Teodor met while working at larger firms, where clients often spoke to a different person every year. In 2011 they rented two rooms above a bookshop on Corliss Street and started Carrack Advisory, named after the sturdy merchant ships that carried everything a long voyage needed.

The practice has stayed small on purpose. Clients meet the planner who writes their plan, fees are set out before any work begins, and every plan is written so it can be read without a glossary.

  • Plain-English plans you keep
  • Fees explained before you engage
  • Coordination with your accountant and attorney
