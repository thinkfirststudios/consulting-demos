Financial planning

A written plan covering cash flow, savings, insurance, retirement and estate questions in one place, reviewed with you line by line.

What it includes

  • Cash flow, saving and spending, set out on one page
  • Insurance and emergency-fund review
  • Retirement, education and estate questions in one written plan

What it does not include

  • Preparing tax returns or drafting legal documents
  • Selling insurance products
Who it is for
Anyone who wants their finances written down and checked by someone who will explain every line.

Investment advice

Advice on how your accounts are invested, set out around the plan and your own timeline rather than the week's headlines.

Retirement planning

  • Pension and Social Security timing questions

What it does not include
